A Closer Look at the Course

Course Overview

The Autism and Emotional Regulation course is a holistic 10-module learning experience created for educators, caregivers, mental health workers, workplace leaders, support professionals, and anyone seeking deeper knowledge about autism. Emotional regulation is one of the core areas of support required by autistic individuals. Without a strong understanding of sensory needs, communication differences, and mental health factors, it becomes challenging to support emotional balance and resilience.

This course aims to bridge that gap by teaching you not only how autism affects emotional development, but how supportive strategies can be implemented across all environments — schools, homes, workplaces, and community settings. Through the 10 modules listed below, you will acquire the confidence, language, and structured tools to better understand emotional needs and respond with evidence-based interventions.

Every module closes with practical prompts you can take straight into a classroom, a care setting or a team meeting, so the learning does not stop at the screen. You will finish with a shared language for emotional regulation and a set of responses that hold up under pressure.
