Can You Reheat Cooked Prawns From the Fridge? A UK Food Safety Guide

If stored and heated correctly, yes. Our UK guide covers safe times, temperatures and methods.

Can You Reheat Cooked Prawns From the Fridge?

Yes, you can reheat cooked prawns from the fridge, provided they were cooled and refrigerated within two hours of cooking, kept below 5°C, and eaten within 48 hours. When reheating, prawns must be heated until steaming hot throughout — ideally reaching a core temperature of at least 70°C for two minutes, or 74°C if you are using a food thermometer. They should only be reheated once.

This is not a blanket “yes” though. Cooked prawns are a ready-to-eat, high-risk food, which means bacteria can multiply on them relatively quickly if they are left in the wrong conditions. The FSA position on leftovers is that food cooled and refrigerated properly can be reheated safely, as long as it is done thoroughly and only once.

Three things to check first

  • They were cooled and refrigerated within two hours of cooking, or of purchase for shop-bought pre-cooked prawns.
  • They have been stored at 8°C or below (ideally 5°C or below) and used within roughly two days.
  • They show no signs of spoilage — no sour smell, sliminess, or discolouration.

How Long Can Cooked Prawns Stay in the Fridge?

Cooked prawns typically last around two days in the fridge when stored at 8°C or below, and ideally at 5°C or below. This applies to both homemade cooked prawns and pre-cooked prawns bought from a supermarket once the pack has been opened. Always check the use-by date on shop-bought products, as this takes priority over general storage guidance.

Fridge, covered0–5°CUp to 2 days
Freezer−18°CUp to 3 months
Room temperatureAbove 8°CNot safe over 2 hours
If in doubt, throw it out. Reheating cannot undo spoilage that has already started.

Best Method to Reheat Cooked Prawns

If your prawns are part of a larger dish, our companion guide, Can You Reheat Prawns? The Complete Guide, covers reheating prawns in curries, stir-fries and pasta bakes in more depth.

  1. Take the prawns out of the fridge and pat them dry.
  2. Heat gently in a pan over a medium heat, turning often.
  3. Check the core temperature reaches 70°C for two minutes.
  4. Serve straight away — do not reheat a second time.
